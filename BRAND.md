# Choose WOW — Brand Guide

## Illustration Style (LOCKED)
**Warm minimalist watercolor editorial illustrations.**

### Prompt template for article hero images:
```
Warm minimalist editorial illustration. Soft watercolor washes in muted gold, terracotta, and cream tones. Simple line art with organic brushstrokes. No text. No people's faces. Evocative and contemplative mood. Style reference: modern editorial illustration meets Japanese wabi-sabi aesthetic. Horizontal 16:9 aspect ratio. [SCENE DESCRIPTION]
```

### Color palette:
- Gold: #c99a2e (primary accent)
- Gold light: #e8b84b
- Cream background: #faf8f3
- Terracotta wash accents
- Dark charcoal text: #1a1a1a

### Key principles:
- No faces (hands okay)
- No text on illustrations (exception: short poetic phrases if integral)
- Warm, contemplative, not corporate
- Objects over people: journals, coffee cups, windows, nature, light
- Watercolor washes as background texture
- Thin line art for objects
- Golden light as recurring motif

## Logo
- Sunrise icon (gold gradient) + "Choose WOW" text
- Favicon: gold rounded square with sunrise/rays

## Typography (web)
- Headings: Space Grotesk (700)
- Body: DM Sans (400/500/600)

## Voice
- See /memory/research/frank-voice-analysis.md

- Not guru, not clinical, not corporate
